Privacy Policy

100% Local

ClickTrap operates strictly on your computer. Webpage content is analyzed locally in memory and is never transmitted to external cloud servers, advertisers, or analytics tracking services.

How to Use ClickTrap

1
Scan Directly on the Web: Enter any website address in the top bar on the "Scan Website" tab and click "Scan Website Now".
2
Or Use the Chrome Extension: Install the extension in Chrome, browse any site, and click the ClickTrap shield icon in your browser toolbar to scan the active tab.
3
Inspect Deceptive Patterns: Review the risk score (0-100), view exact quote excerpts, and read consumer advice on how to protect your money and data.
